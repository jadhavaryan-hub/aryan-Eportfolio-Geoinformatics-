Remote Sensing & Field Techniques • Capstone

Pashan Lake: Vegetation, Invasive Species & Restoration Assessment

Evaluated decadal LULC change (35.1% built-up expansion) and water hyacinth (Eichhornia crassipes) spread across Pashan Lake using Sentinel-2, Landsat-8, LISS-IV, and Epicollect5 mobile field transects.

Study Area: Pashan Lake, Pune, Maharashtra
Datasets: Sentinel-2, Landsat-8, LISS-IV, Epicollect5 GPS
Tools: ArcGIS Pro, Google Earth Engine, Random Forest
Key Result: Restoration Priority Zoning Map & Policy Brief
